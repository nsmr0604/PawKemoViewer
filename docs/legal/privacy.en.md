# PawKemoViewer Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-26
Last updated: 2026-11-26

This Privacy Policy explains how PawKemoViewer ("the App") handles user information.

## 1. Information stored

The App stores the following **only on your device**:

- Settings (theme, view mode, language, network timeout, etc.)
- Follows, favorites, and read state
- Credentials you enter (a pawchive session key)
- Caches such as post thumbnails and creator icons
- App logs (on-device)
- Your 18+ confirmation state

The App does not collect your name, address, phone number, location, contacts, or advertising ID. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

## 2. Purposes

Stored information is used to:

1. Provide App features (posts, creators, favorites, follows)
2. Preserve and restore settings
3. Investigate issues (only when you choose to share logs)

## 3. External communication

The App communicates with:

- **pawchive.pw**: to fetch API data and images. Your credentials are used only for this communication.
- **FANBOX / Patreon and other external pages**: when you open an external page, it is displayed in a WebView at its real origin.
- **GitHub**: for links to the Terms and this Policy, and update checks.

Communications use encrypted channels such as HTTPS. The developer does not operate a server that collects or stores your information.

## 4. Third-party disclosure

The App does not disclose your information to third parties except as required by law. The services listed above receive information necessary to provide their functionality, subject to their own privacy policies.

## 5. Logs

The App writes logs on your device. Credentials (session keys), cookies, and Authorization headers are not logged by design. Review logs before sharing them.

## 6. Retention and deletion

1. Stored information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App.
2. You can reset or delete data from the settings screen.
3. Backup files are saved wherever you choose. Credentials are not included in backups by design.

## 7. Security

The App takes measures such as avoiding logging or clipboard copying of credentials. The security of your device (OS updates, screen lock, etc.) is your responsibility.

## 8. Minors

The App is intended only for users aged 18 or older. Do not use it if you are under 18.

## 9. Access, correction, and deletion requests

Because the developer does not store user information on a server, the information subject to such requests is generally limited to your device. Contact us with any questions:

- General: https://github.com/nsmr0604/PawKemoViewer/issues
- Privacy: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}

## 10. Changes

This Policy may change as needed. Changes take effect when posted in the App or in the repository.
